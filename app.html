Get Delegance Desk

Desk installs straight from your browser on every device. It is free, there is no app store, and it updates itself. Desk is for the Delegance team: sign in with the email your team owner added.

Install on this device

Open Desk, sign in, then follow the steps for your device below.

Open Delegance Desk
Scan with your phone camera to open Desk there

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open desk.delegance.app in Safari and sign in.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up). On newer iPhones it sits inside the ••• menu.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. Desk appears on your home screen.

Android

  1. Open desk.delegance.app in Chrome and sign in.
  2. Tap Install app when it pops up, or tap the ⋮ menu (top right).
  3. Tap Add to home screen, then Install.
  4. On Samsung Internet: tap ≡, then Add page to → Home screen.

Windows, Chromebook and Linux

  1. Open desk.delegance.app in Chrome or Edge and sign in.
  2. Chrome: click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, then Install.
  3. Edge: click … → Apps → Install this site as an app.
  4. Desk gets its own window, Start menu entry and taskbar icon.

Mac

  1. Chrome or Edge: same as Windows, use the install icon in the address bar.
  2. Safari (macOS Sonoma or newer): File → Add to Dock → Add.
  3. Desk opens from the Dock and Launchpad like any app.

Already installed? Desk updates by itself. When a new version is ready you will see a Reload button inside the app.
