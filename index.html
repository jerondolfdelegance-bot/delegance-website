Features

Everything social, in one place.

Stop jumping between apps and tabs. Delegance gives your team one screen to publish, listen and respond.

Compose once

Write a post a single time and publish it to every connected page and profile, adjusted for each network.

Plan ahead

Schedule posts on a calendar and see what goes out, when, and where, at a glance.

The Front Desk

Every comment and message from every network lands in one inbox, so nothing slips through the cracks.

AI replies, on your terms

The assistant learns your business from a simple knowledge sheet and drafts answers to common questions. Start in draft mode and approve every reply with one tap, then switch chosen topics to automatic once you trust it. Complaints and anything it isn't sure about always wait for a person.

Website chat

Add a small chat bubble to your website. Visitor questions join the same Front Desk and get the same quick answers.

How it works

Three steps to calm.

Connect your accounts

Sign in to each network once through its official login. Delegance never sees or stores your passwords.

Teach it your business

Fill in a short knowledge sheet: services, hours, contact details and common questions.

Post and reply from one desk

Publish everywhere, then work through one tidy inbox with replies already drafted.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Delegance available yet?

Not yet. Delegance is in development and this site will be updated as features go live.

Which networks will it support?

Facebook Pages, Instagram, YouTube and LinkedIn, plus a chat bubble for your own website. LinkedIn Company Pages will follow once LinkedIn approves access.

Will the AI reply without asking me?

Only if you switch that on. By default every AI reply is a draft you approve, edit or skip. You can make chosen topics automatic later, and anything unusual always waits for a person.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Delegance runs in your web browser. You can also install it from your browser as a desktop app, using the same account.

Does Delegance store my social media passwords?

No. You sign in through each network's own login page, and the network gives Delegance permission to act for you. You can remove that permission at any time.
