FAQ

Questions, answered.

What kind of work do you take on?

Virtual assistant support, websites, web and mobile apps, ongoing site care, integrations and the everyday tasks that come with running a business. If you're not sure whether something fits, just ask.

Is there anything you don't take on?

Yes. We don't take on work that calls for a licensed professional, such as medical, legal or bookkeeping tasks, or anything in high-risk fields. We'd rather point you to the right specialist.

Can you look after a website someone else built?

Usually, yes. We'll take a look first and let you know honestly what we can do and what we'd recommend.

Do you only build new things?

No. Plenty of our work is improving, fixing and maintaining what you already have.

How do we get started?

Email us at admin@delegance.app with a few lines about what you need. We'll reply with questions or a suggested next step.

What is Delegance Desk?

A social media inbox we're building: one place to post, read comments and messages, and reply with help from AI drafts. It isn't available yet.
