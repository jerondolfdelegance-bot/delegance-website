What is an AI Delegate?

An AI Delegate is a trained person who runs AI tools and agents for your business. The AI drafts, sorts, researches and keeps things moving around the clock, and your Delegate guides it, fixes what it gets wrong and checks every step before anything reaches your customers. You get the speed of AI with a real person accountable for the result.

What kind of work do you take on?

AI Delegate support, websites, web and mobile apps, ongoing site care, integrations and the everyday tasks that come with running a business. If you're not sure whether something fits, just ask.

Is there anything you don't take on?

Yes. We don't take on work that calls for a licensed professional, such as medical, legal or bookkeeping tasks, or anything in high-risk fields. We'd rather point you to the right specialist.

Can I choose my AI Delegate myself?

Yes. Browse every Delegate on the candidates page, save the ones you like, compare up to three side by side and message them directly. Or book a call and we will shortlist the right people for you.

How do I sign in without a password?

Add a passkey in Account settings and sign in with your fingerprint, face or phone PIN. Forgot your password? Choose "Email me a sign-in code" on the sign-in page.

How do you protect my information?

Contact details stay private until both sides sign an agreement, private details are stored encrypted, and you get an email whenever your account signs in from a new device. You can download your data any time. Read our privacy policy.

Can you look after a website someone else built?

Usually, yes. We'll take a look first and let you know honestly what we can do and what we'd recommend.

Do you only build new things?

No. Plenty of our work is improving, fixing and maintaining what you already have.

How do we get started?

Email us at admin@delegance.app with a few lines about what you need, or tap the chat button in the corner. We'll reply with questions or a suggested next step.

What is Delegance Desk?

The tool our team uses to run our clients' social media: planning and scheduling posts, and answering comments and messages with help from AI drafts that a person checks first. Clients don't need to install anything.

Do you need my social media passwords?

No. You add us to your page through the network's own page settings, and you can remove our access at any time.
