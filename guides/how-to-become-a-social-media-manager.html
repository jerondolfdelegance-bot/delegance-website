How to become a social media manager

A social media manager helps a business show up consistently: planning posts, creating simple content, answering comments and learning what works. It is a natural next step for many virtual assistants.

What the job includes

  • Planning: a monthly content calendar with themes, dates and offers.
  • Creating: captions, simple graphics and short videos in the brand's style.
  • Publishing: scheduling posts at the right times for each platform.
  • Community: replying to comments and messages kindly and quickly.
  • Reporting: a short monthly summary of what worked and what to try next.

Skills to practise

  1. Writing short captions with a clear first line and one call to action.
  2. Basic design: consistent fonts, colours and spacing.
  3. Short video editing: cutting, captions and music that is licensed for use.
  4. Reading simple numbers: reach, saves, shares, link clicks and comments.

Build a portfolio without a client

Pick a real local business you like and create a sample two-week plan: six posts, captions, and one short video. Label it clearly as a practice sample, not paid work. Put it on your Delegance profile as a portfolio project.

A simple weekly routine

Monday: plan the week, confirm offers and dates
Tuesday–Wednesday: create and schedule posts
Daily: reply to comments and messages twice a day
Friday: note what performed best and why

Good habits that keep clients

Get approval before posting anything sensitive, keep brand files organised in the client's storage, and never use music, images or logos you do not have the right to use.
